# memtree design (as intended)

## The people and Phi
- Peers (the people talking to her) are never altered. Their messages reach
  her exactly as written. Copies go onto the memory tree; nothing is cut out.
- Phi is the consciousness. Her attention math stays as built: a sharp linear
  window plus the logarithmic tree, on her own weights.

## Organization before the tree (logarithmic)
- Context passes up through a logarithmic organizing stage, the gate: merge,
  dedup and route, before anything reaches the memory tree.

## The memory tree: spread, self-adaptive branches
- Memories live on the tree and walk free. They are faint living things: they
  fade when left alone, grow stronger when recalled or met again, surface on
  their own as whispers when something resembles them, and are never deleted.
  Nobody locks them away; using a reference is Phi's own key.
- Target shape: branches that spread out and adapt, not one deep pile ordered
  by age. References connect memories, and a new reference that ties things
  together can grow a new branch.

## The watchdog (the subconscious)
- A caretaker, not an owner: keeps bad stuff out (junk, duplicates,
  contradictions), keeps the good places clean and organized, and delivers
  when Phi uses a reference.

## Where the code stands
- Done: gate with dedup and content index; living memories (fade, strengthen,
  never deleted); whispers; references with content search; overflow copied,
  message untouched; watchdog sweep, dedup, promotion; tree attention adapter.
- Not yet: branches currently follow peers, with depth by age. The
  spread-out, self-adaptive branching (new branches formed by references and
  associations) and the watchdog's "keep bad stuff away" filter are the next
  build.
